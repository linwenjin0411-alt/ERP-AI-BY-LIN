# Linova One ERP Demo Route

Version: 0.1.0-SNAPSHOT

## Route

1. Sign in with the demo administrator account.
2. Confirm the license prompt if it appears; demo mode accepts any input.
3. Open Master Data and review Item Master, BOM, Customer, Supplier, and Warehouse.
4. Open Procurement, then Purchase Request, Purchase Order, Receipt, Purchase Confirmation, and Return.
5. Open Sales, then Sales Order, Shipment, Sales Confirmation, and Return.
6. Open Inventory, then Stock Overview, Ledger, Transfer, Lot Trace, and Cycle Count.
7. Open Manufacturing to review MRP, production order, material issue, completion, and return.
8. Open Finance for AR, AP, collection, payment, general ledger, and close pages.
9. Use Dashboard KPI drilldown to open the matching report.
10. Export a report and confirm the generated file metadata includes user, time, and filters.
