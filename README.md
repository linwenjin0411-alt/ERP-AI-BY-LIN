# Intelligent Enterprise ERP

A modern, modular Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform designed for manufacturing companies and enterprise business operations.

The project is inspired by enterprise ERP solutions such as SAP and mcframe, with a focus on **manufacturing management, supply chain integration, inventory control, sales, procurement, financial operations, and data-driven decision making**.

In addition to traditional ERP capabilities, the platform introduces **AI-assisted automation and intelligent business insights** to improve operational efficiency and help users understand enterprise data more easily.

> **Status:** 🚧 Under active development

---

## 🚀 Overview

Modern manufacturing companies often operate across multiple disconnected systems for sales, procurement, production, inventory, and finance.

This project aims to provide an integrated ERP platform that connects these business processes through a unified data model and modular architecture.

The system is designed around several core principles:

* Modular ERP architecture
* Manufacturing-oriented business processes
* Integrated enterprise data
* Real-time inventory and transaction tracking
* Configurable workflows
* Role-based access control
* REST API integration
* Multi-language support
* AI-assisted business operations
* Extensible architecture for enterprise customization

## 🤖 AI-Assisted Features

The project explores practical ways to integrate AI into traditional ERP workflows.

AI is designed as an **assistant to enterprise users**, rather than replacing deterministic ERP business logic.

Potential AI capabilities include:

### Natural Language ERP Query

Users may query business data using natural language.

Example:

> "Which products are currently below their safety stock level?"

The system can translate the request into structured queries and present relevant ERP data.

### Intelligent Business Insights

AI can analyze operational data and highlight important information such as:

* Inventory anomalies
* Unusual sales trends
* Delayed purchase orders
* Potential material shortages
* Production bottlenecks

### AI Report Summary

AI can generate concise summaries from ERP reports and dashboards.

For example:

> "Sales increased this month, but inventory turnover decreased due to higher stock levels in Warehouse A."

### AI-Assisted Workflow

AI may assist users with repetitive enterprise tasks such as:

* Drafting purchase requests
* Summarizing production results
* Explaining abnormal transactions
* Searching enterprise knowledge
* Suggesting follow-up actions

## 🎯 Project Vision

The goal of this project is to explore how a traditional enterprise ERP platform can evolve into a more intelligent and user-friendly business system.

Instead of treating AI as a standalone feature, the project aims to integrate AI directly into enterprise workflows while keeping critical ERP transactions **structured, traceable, and deterministic**.

The long-term vision is to build a flexible ERP foundation capable of supporting manufacturing companies from basic business management to advanced production and supply-chain operations.
